New Funnel Section
Product Adoption
AI Discover Page (Admin Console)
Challenge
While Box had invested heavily in AI launches, thought leadership, webinars, and educational content, many administrators still lacked a clear path from awareness to activation.

The challenge wasn't awareness.

The challenge was helping admins understand:

What Box AI could do
Why it mattered
How to get started
inside the product experience itself.

Approach
Partner with Product and Design to create an in-product destination that connected AI education directly to activation.

Rather than treating content and enablement as separate from the product experience, bring AI learning directly into the Admin Console where decision makers already worked.

Action
Collaborated closely with Product and Design to shape the AI Discover Page through a PMM and adoption lens.

Key contributions included:

Defining the messaging hierarchy
Designing the educational journey
Integrating AI Explainer Series content
Integrating AI in Action customer stories
Advocating for keeping videos embedded in-product
Aligning the experience with broader AI education and enablement programs
Influencing content strategy and placement decisions based on expected adoption impact
The goal was to create a single in-product destination that helped admins move from curiosity to activation.

Results
Adoption Impact
~45% AI enablement rate among Discover Page users vs. ~25% baseline
~2× higher AI enablement
~4× higher weekly AI usage
6.4% incremental lift in AI enablement
~750 additional enterprises enabled beyond organic growth
Activation Impact
Customers exposed to Discover:

Activated AI significantly faster
Showed stronger long-term adoption
Demonstrated 2–5× higher AI engagement across customer segments
Strategic Impact
Established a scalable in-product channel for AI education, customer adoption, and content distribution.

Key Learning
One of the biggest lessons from AI GTM is that customers don't separate "education" from "product."

The highest-performing adoption experiences often happen when learning is embedded directly into the workflow rather than delivered through external campaigns.

The AI Discover Page reinforced that in-product education can be one of the most effective growth levers available to product marketers.
