Case Study Title
Secure RAG: Turning AI Trust into Adoption

Subheader
Created Box AI's foundational Secure RAG architecture narrative and technical explainer, helping customers understand how enterprise AI could be deployed securely, accelerating sales conversations, AI activation, and customer confidence.

Challenge

As enterprise interest in AI accelerated, customers increasingly wanted to understand exactly how Box AI worked. Security leaders, architects, and administrators needed a clear explanation of how enterprise data remained protected within AI workflows, but a lack of simple, customer-facing technical guidance was creating friction in sales cycles, AI activation, and ongoing adoption.

Approach

Partnered with Product leadership and the lead Box AI architect to translate complex technical architecture into a customer-friendly narrative centered on Secure RAG. The goal was to build trust by making Box's AI architecture transparent, understandable, and differentiated from competing approaches.

Actions
Defined and developed the Secure RAG positioning and core customer narrative.
Authored the end-to-end whitepaper and technical explainer.
Designed the architecture flow and worked with Brand to transform it into a polished customer-facing asset.
Aligned Product, Engineering, Architecture, Sales, and Executive stakeholders around a unified AI trust story.
Enabled field teams to confidently address security, governance, and architecture questions during enterprise AI evaluations.
Results
Delivered one of the most requested AI sales and customer education assets.
Reduced friction in enterprise AI discussions by helping security, IT, and executive stakeholders understand how Box AI protects customer data.
Supported AI activation and adoption efforts by increasing administrator confidence in enabling Box AI.
Strengthened Box's differentiation in enterprise AI through a clear and defensible Secure RAG narrative.
Received recognition from executive leadership, including Box's COO, for driving the initiative from concept through execution.
