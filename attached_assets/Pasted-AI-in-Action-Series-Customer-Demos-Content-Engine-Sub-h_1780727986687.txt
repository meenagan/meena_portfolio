AI in Action Series (Customer Demos & Content Engine)

Sub-header
Built and scaled a consistent AI demo and content engine showcasing real-world Box AI use cases, enabling Product and Marketing teams to communicate AI value through practical, repeatable storytelling.

Challenge

AI demos were inconsistent and slow to produce, limiting the ability to rapidly showcase new capabilities in a fast-moving AI landscape.

Approach

Created a structured demo framework and repeatable content system to accelerate production and improve consistency across use cases.

Actions
Defined AI in Action demo structure and narrative format
Partnered with Product teams to identify key use cases
Standardized demo storytelling across verticals
Enabled multiple contributors beyond core PMM team
Operationalized demo publishing cadence
Results
Increased velocity of AI demo content creation
Improved consistency of product storytelling
Enabled broader cross-functional contribution
Strengthened AI narrative across customer touchpoints
Supported faster go-to-market cycles for new features
Key Learning

Product storytelling scales best when it is systematized—consistent structure enables speed without sacrificing narrative clarity or technical accuracy.

5. AI Certification & Customer Education Programs

Sub-header
Developed structured AI education programs to help customers and internal teams understand and apply Box AI capabilities across real-world workflows.

Challenge

Customers needed structured guidance to move from AI awareness to practical usage across daily workflows.

Approach

Designed scalable education formats that combined structured learning with applied AI use cases.

Actions
Built AI certification and enablement content
Developed “AI in Action” learning modules
Created hands-on workshop formats
Aligned content with real customer workflows
Partnered with Sales and CS for adoption enablement
Results
Improved customer understanding of Box AI capabilities
Increased adoption readiness among administrators
Strengthened enablement across field teams
Created repeatable education motion for AI adoption
Supported onboarding and activation journeys
Key Learning

AI education is most effective when it is tied directly to workflows—customers engage more deeply when learning is immediately applicable.
